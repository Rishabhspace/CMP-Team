Conference Management Portal

IIT Roorkee's comprehensive solution for academic conference management

Project Overview

The Conference Management Portal is a centralized web application tailored for the academic needs of IIT Roorkee, designed to streamline conference organization and participation.

Paper Submission

Easy upload system for abstracts and full papers with version tracking

Review System

Comprehensive review workflow with customizable criteria and feedback

Payment Integration

Secure payment gateway for registration fees and additional services

E-Certificate Generation

Automated creation of participation and presentation certificates

Email Notifications

Automated alerts for deadlines, reviews, and important updates

Support System

Real-time chat and ticketing system for attendee assistance
