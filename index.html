🚀 Project Overview

The Conference Management Portal is a centralized web application tailored for the needs of IIT Roorkee. It supports:

  • 📥 Paper/Abstract Submission
  • 📝 Review & Approval System
  • 💳 Payment Integration (if applicable)
  • 📄 Certificate Generation
  • 📧 Automated Email Notifications
  • 💬 Chat & Support Feature for Attendees

From organizing paper tracks to sending notifications — CMP simplifies it all.
